This Privacy Policy describes how Kosho Sakae Auto Post ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our TikTok content automation service at https://kosho-sakae.github.io/kosho-auto-post/.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Authenticating your TikTok account within this application

  • Automating video publishing to your TikTok profile via Make.com integration workflows

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties other than those necessary to operate the automation workflow (Make.com, Creatomate).

3. Data Storage and Retention

Kosho Sakae Auto Post does not maintain its own backend database. OAuth access tokens obtained from TikTok are stored within your Make.com account's data stores and are never stored on our servers. Your TikTok user data is only held in the browser session for display purposes and is not persisted after you sign out.

You may revoke access at any time by using the "Revoke TikTok Access" option in the app's Settings page, or directly from your TikTok account's connected apps settings.
